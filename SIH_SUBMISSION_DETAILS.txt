FIELD 5: ABSTRACT / SUMMARY
================================================================================
[Limit: Max 10,000 characters]

ThermoTwin is an enterprise-grade, physics-informed AI digital twin engineered specifically for Oil India Limited's (OIL) extreme heavy-oil assets (Baghewala Field, Bikaner-Nagaur Basin, Rajasthan). Producing extra-heavy crude (16°–19° API) with ultra-high reservoir viscosities (>15,000 cP) requires continuous Cyclic Steam Stimulation (CSS / Huff-and-Puff) combined with Sucker Rod Pumping (SRP). Existing operations suffer from disconnected, reactive workflows—reservoir thermal cycles and surface pump speeds are tuned in isolation, resulting in frequent rod floating, fluid pound, high rod-parting rates, elevated Steam-to-Oil Ratios (SOR), and costly field downtime.

ThermoTwin solves this by creating a closed-loop, 3-layer synchronized digital twin bridging:
1. Reservoir Layer: Models steam dissipation, thermal radius, and temperature-viscosity decay using calibrated ASTM D341 equations.
2. Wellbore Layer: Computes wellbore heat loss and dynamic flowing fluid gradient to the downhole pump.
3. Surface / SRP Layer: Synthesizes real-time 36-point surface and downhole dynamometer cards via the Gibbs damped wave equation, modeling rod stress, pump fillage, and VFD SPM settings.

Our dual-core Machine Learning engine deploys an unsupervised Isolation Forest for multi-sensor anomaly detection (flagging rod float and pump unsetting in <250 ms) and a Random Forest Regressor optimizing VFD stroke speed (SPM) to maximize production while preventing fatigue failures. Explainability is delivered via SHAP TreeExplainer waterfall plots so operators understand the exact physical root cause behind every recommendation.

ThermoTwin includes an interactive "What-If" Simulation Sandbox, an AI Operations Copilot, and an automated compliance reporting engine. The system is fully operational with 17/17 automated test suites passing and deployed on live edge infrastructure (FastAPI backend + React/Vite industrial SCADA UI).

Projected Field Impact:
• Operational: Up to 75% reduction in unplanned well downtime (from 18–24 down to 4–6 days/well/year); card diagnosis time cut from 4–8 hours to <250 ms.
• Economic: Rs 1.2–1.8 Crore OPEX savings per well cluster annually; 12–14.5% overall lifting energy reduction.
• Environmental/ESG: ~18% lower steam-generation operational costs, lower Steam-Oil Ratio (SOR), and reduced carbon footprint.

1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & PROBLEM STATEMENT
In the Thar Desert (Baghewala Field, Rajasthan), Oil India Limited operates challenging heavy-oil reservoirs containing 16°–19° API crude with initial viscosities exceeding 15,000 cP. To produce this bitumen-like fluid, operators use Cyclic Steam Stimulation (CSS) to heat the reservoir and reduce oil viscosity to pumpable levels (<45 cP), lifted to surface via Sucker Rod Pump (SRP) beam units.

Currently, CSS thermal injection and SRP surface lifting operate as disconnected silos:
- Reservoir engineers tune steam volume, soak time, and cycle cut-offs based on historical averages.
- Field operators adjust SRP stroke speed (SPM) reactively only after mechanical failures (such as rod float, pump unsetting, fluid pound, or parted rods) trigger shutdowns.
- As the steam zone cools post-injection, fluid viscosity spikes rapidly. If pump speed is not dynamically synchronized, severe downstroke drag occurs, causing rod compression, buckling, and expensive workover interventions.

ThermoTwin transforms this reactive paradigm into a predictive, physics-informed, data-driven closed loop.


2. PROPOSED SOLUTION: 3-LAYER SYNCHRONIZED DIGITAL TWIN
ThermoTwin couples downhole petro-physics with surface mechanics across three real-time layers:

A. Reservoir Layer (Thermal & Inflow Dynamics):
- Tracks the injected steam bubble propagation, heat dissipation, and conductive heating radius.
- Models exponential viscosity collapse during the 3 CSS lifecycle stages (Steam Injection, Soaking, and Production Lift).

B. Wellbore Layer (Hydraulic & Thermal Transfer):
- Models fluid temperature and pressure profiles along the wellbore to the pump intake.
- Evaluates friction losses and thermal transfer to surrounding formations.

C. Surface / Sucker Rod Pump Layer (Kinematics & Loading):
- Solves the Gibbs 1D damped wave equation to project surface polished rod loads downhole to the pump plunger.
- Generates 36-point diagnostic dynamometer cards in real-time, detecting pump fillage percentage and downhole valve anomalies.

Coupled AI Optimization Engine:
Instead of isolated adjustments, ThermoTwin continuously co-optimizes CSS parameters (steam volume, soak duration, cycle cut-off) alongside SRP operational parameters (VFD stroke speed, polished rod loads).


3. MATHEMATICAL & PHYSICS-INFORMED FOUNDATIONS
• Gibbs Damped Wave Equation for Rod Mechanics:
  d^2 u(x,t) / dt^2 = a^2 * (d^2 u(x,t) / dx^2) - nu * (du(x,t) / dt)
  Projects elastic wave propagation through high-tensile steel sucker rod strings (wave velocity a approx 4,900 m/s, damping factor nu accounting for heavy-crude drag) to identify fluid pound, gas interference, and parted rods.

• ASTM D341 Viscosity-Temperature Model:
  log10 log10 (nu + 0.7) = A - B * log10(T)
  Calibrated specifically on Baghewala 17°–19° API fluid parameters, accurately tracking viscosity decay from 15,000 cP at 25°C down to 45 cP at 120°C.

• Unsupervised Multi-Sensor Anomaly Detection:
  Uses an Isolation Forest ensemble evaluating multi-dimensional telemetry vectors (load, SPM, motor temperature, vibration RMS, casing pressure) with contamination scoring to flag developing mechanical abnormalities without needing pre-labeled failure datasets.

• Explainable AI (XAI) with SHAP:
  Computes game-theoretic Shapley attribution values (SHAP TreeExplainer) to generate waterfall plots for field operators, displaying the exact percentage contribution of each telemetry parameter to the system's operational recommendations.


4. TECHNICAL ARCHITECTURE & STACK
• Backend & API Layer:
  - Framework: FastAPI (Python 3.12) high-throughput REST gateway with CORS and automatic Swagger/OpenAPI documentation.
  - Database: Managed PostgreSQL 16 storing time-series sensor telemetry, asset registry metadata, and SCADA alarm audit trails.
  - SCADA Simulator: Asynchronous multi-well simulator generating continuous telemetry with realistic noise and sensor drift.

• Machine Learning & Scientific Computing:
  - Scikit-Learn (RandomForestRegressor for SPM optimization, IsolationForest for anomaly detection).
  - SHAP & Plotly for explainability and interactive dynamometer plotting.
  - NumPy & SciPy for wave equation numerical integration and ASTM D341 solvers.

• Frontend & Industrial Control Console:
  - Framework: React 19 + Vite single-page application with glassmorphic SCADA UI.
  - Interactive Visualizations: Real-time kinematic 2D SVG beam pump animator ("Nodding Donkey") synchronized with live SPM, radial thermal heat-dispersion graphics, and interactive What-If Simulation Sandbox.
  - AI Operations Copilot: Domain-specific petroleum operations assistant grounded in live database telemetry with interactive guided decision-tree chips.
